PROCESS STUDY AT UIN JAKARTA
1. Contact Center for International Cooperation UIN Jakarta
2. Decide on degree and program & faculties/department
3. Upload the required documents at: https://bit.ly/ADFORMUIN
4. Announcement of Acceptance
5. Apply for a study permit and student visa
6. Join in a language program (BIPA)
7. Travel and study at UIN Jakarta


SCHOLARSHIP AVAILABLE
Admission Requirements for International Students
Undergraduate Program (S1)
1. Current Curriculum Vitae (CV)
2. Colored Photo 4 x 6 (with red background)
3. High School Certificate (Max 3 years after graduation)
4. High School Transcript
5. Institutional TOEFL score of at least 500 or TOAFL score of at least 450 or IELTS 6.0 or BIPA certificate
6. Health Certificate (Medical Check-Up)
7. Evidence of sufficient funds, such as personal bank statements min USD 2000
8. Cover Passport (min 18 months before expired)

Graduate Program (M.A. and Ph.D.)
1. Colored photo 4 x 6 (with red background)
2. Undergraduate/Bachelor certificate and master certificate for doctorate applicants
3. Undergraduate/Bachelor certificate and master transcripts for doctorate applicants
4. Institutional TOEFL score of at least 500 or TOAFL score of at least 450 or IELTS 6.0 or BIPA certificate
5. Recommendation Letter from at least two previous teachers/Professor(s)
6. Health Certificate (Medical Check Up)
7. Permit Letter from the institution you work (if applicable)
8. Research Proposal (4-8 pages consist of background, literature review & methods)
9. Evidence of sufficient funds, such as personal bank statements minimum last 3 months min USD 2000
10. Cover Passport (min 18 months before expired)
